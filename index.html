§ 01 About

Hi, I'm Alex. I’m a mechanical engineering graduate from the University of Vermont with hands-on experience in CAD design, embedded systems, and prototyping. I enjoy working between the CAD model and the physical build, where real-world constraints force the design to become smarter, simpler, and more reliable. This site is a record of that process: the projects, the internships, and the work that taught me how engineering actually feels outside the classroom.

Strengths

  • Comfortable turning rough ideas into physical systems and working prototypes
  • Strong CAD and design-for-manufacture thinking in SolidWorks and Revit
  • Effective at embedded sensing, prototyping, and iteration under real constraints
  • Enjoys troubleshooting systems and using data to improve performance

Weaknesses

  • Occasionally learns a lesson the hard way during the first prototype
  • Can get a little too committed to an idea before testing it in the real world
  • Still occasionally over-thinks a design before the build is done
  • Has a personal grudge against unnecessary complexity in any system
