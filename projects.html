Miniaturized Wireless Temperature Logger for Aquatic Research

Engineered a sub-0.8g wearable sensor designed to continuously monitor underwater temperature with pinpoint accuracy. The system logs measurements every 30 minutes and transmits data wirelessly via RFID, eliminating recovery loss. Achieved ±0.5°C accuracy through algorithmic calibration and chamber validation, integrating a microcontroller, precision sensor, and embedded antenna into a form factor small enough for vulnerable aquatic species.

Artemis II RS-25 Engine Performance

Conducted comprehensive computational fluid dynamics analysis of the RS-25 rocket engine across three expansion ratio configurations, leveraging actual Artemis II mission data. Built validated ANSYS Fluent models capturing chamber thermodynamics, nozzle flow expansion, and atmospheric interaction. Study focused on transient plume development and thermal-structural efficiency, providing insights into engine performance under realistic launch conditions and validating design margins for future missions.
